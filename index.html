RhythmIO

It's about time.

An intuitive media editor built for the rhythms of creative flow.

The old lines between slideshow, video and music edit are gone, and so is the old pace. RhythmIO puts pictures, video and sound on one timeline and lets you set the beat. Rough it in, then tune it up: work fast or work meticulously, as a beginner or a pro, with settings that make the workflow your own. Slide decks, music videos, YouTube shows, podcasts, milestone montages, the awards-night reel of everyone's most embarrassing moments. The list is endless and up to you.

BGTools · free to start

Hot-rod your desktop.

Expand the desktop background capabilities for your Mac. BGTools lets you set the timing to the second, mix in video, and run a different show on every display and Space, behind your icons or in a window up front, or sync them all up into a single multi-monitor tour de force. It even plays shows you've created with RhythmIO. Whether it's a comforting collection of family photos, the peaceful meditation of your favorite vistas, or the hectic cyberpunk light show you're after, BGTools has got you covered.

UP Scaler · free to start

Enjoy the show.

Batch processing can be a tedious chore. UP is here to shake things, well, up. Drop your undersized files into the playlist, and UP automatically checks which ones really need upsizing and starts working through your stack. You can compare render engines side by side, rearrange the stack, decide which images need to be saved, and output the stack in your hand-picked order. Press play and watch your stack as a slideshow while the batch processes. And it all happens locally, on your Mac. Your images always stay on your hard drive, internet not required!
