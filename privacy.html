Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

At Qdeco, we believe tracking users is a fundamental violation of privacy. This policy outlines how our applications, and this website, handle information.

1. Data collection

Our applications do not collect, store, transmit, or harvest any personal information, usage analytics, or device data. All application states and configurations remain strictly on your local device hardware.

2. Third-party services

We do not integrate third-party analytics frameworks, advertising networks, or data brokers into our software builds.

3. Purchases

Our apps are sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles every purchase, and we never see your payment details.

4. Support inquiries

If you reach out via our support email channel, we will only use your email address to correspond with you and resolve your specific technical request. We will never sell or share your contact information.

5. This website

  • No cookies, no analytics and no tracking.
  • If you change the reading width or the page background, your browser remembers that choice in its own local storage. It never leaves your device, and clearing your browser's site data erases it.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, to run and secure its service. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • The main page loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, so your browser asks Google for them. This page uses only your device's own fonts.

6. Contact us

For questions regarding this simple privacy approach, contact us at support@qdeco.com.
