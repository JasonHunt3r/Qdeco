Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

At Qdeco, we believe tracking users is a fundamental violation of privacy. This policy outlines how our applications handle information.

1. Data Collection

Our applications do not collect, store, transmit, or harvest any personal information, usage analytics, or device data. All application states and configurations remain strictly on your local device hardware.

2. Third-Party Services

We do not integrate third-party analytics frameworks, advertising networks, or data brokers into our software builds.

3. Support Inquiries

If you reach out via our support email channel, we will only use your email address to correspond with you and resolve your specific technical request. We will never sell or share your contact information.

4. Contact Us

For questions regarding this simple privacy approach, contact us at support@qdeco.com.
